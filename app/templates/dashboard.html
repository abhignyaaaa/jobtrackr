How these are calculated
  • Submitted (the denominator): applications whose status history includes any status other than Saved.
  • Reached interview: submitted applications that were ever marked Interview or have an interview recorded.
  • Received an offer: submitted applications that were ever marked Offer Received.
  • Awaiting response: current status is Applied. Applied this month: application date in the current calendar month.
